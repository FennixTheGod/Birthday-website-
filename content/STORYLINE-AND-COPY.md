## Memory gallery
Use captions that are short and personal:
- “A little moment I kept.”
- “The kind of day I wish I could replay.”
- “Proof that the smallest moments can mean the most.”
- “Our little memory, forever in my favorites.”
Replace each caption with a real description and date where appropriate.

## Quiz
Create editable multiple-choice questions in the content file. Use placeholder questions until the user adds true answers:
- “Which moment would I happily relive?”
- “What always makes us laugh?”
- “Which little detail do I notice about you?”
Do not claim that placeholder answers are factual.

## Candle moment

**Instruction:** Take a breath and blow toward your microphone — or tap the button if you prefer.  
**After candles go out:** May this year bring you gentle days, brave dreams, real laughter, and every beautiful thing you deserve.

## Love letter
**Heading:** A little letter for you  
**Starter message:** I may not always find the perfect words, but I hope you always know this: your presence can make an ordinary day feel special. Thank you for the conversations, the laughs, the little memories, and all the moments that mean more than they seem. Today, I hope you feel celebrated, appreciated, and loved. Happy birthday, [HER_NAME].  
Personalize this message so it sounds like the sender.
